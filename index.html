🏥 Healthcare / Dental Clinic 6-Month Campaign

Local Dental Clinic Grows Organic Appointments by 180%

Problem

A dental clinic in Saharanpur was invisible on Google. Competitors ranked for all high-intent local queries like "dentist near me" and "teeth whitening Saharanpur." Monthly organic traffic was below 200 visitors with zero map-pack presence.

Strategy

Full technical audit fixing crawl errors + page speed. Optimised 12 service pages with geo-targeted keywords. Claimed and fully optimised Google Business Profile. Built 20 local citations and earned 8 authority backlinks from health directories.

🛒 E-commerce / Home Decor 4-Month Campaign

Home Decor Store Doubles Revenue with Organic Search

Problem

An online home décor store was spending heavily on Google Ads with thin margins. Their organic channel had high potential but product pages were thin, image ALT tags were missing, and category pages had duplicate content issues causing cannibilisation.

Strategy

Consolidated duplicate content, rewrote 30 product pages with SEO-rich copy, fixed schema markup for products and breadcrumbs, created a topical blog cluster around interior design trends, and built editorial backlinks from 3 home décor publications.

Results

+210% Organic Traffic
+95% Organic Revenue

-60% Ad Spend
